The daily loop · specialists

Clock in → Today → Start Work

Clock in→Read Today top-down→Start Work on card 1→Tick steps→Submit or Park (why)→Next card→Clock out

The five habits

  • One card at a time. The green strip at the top is your running timer; switching cards pauses the old one.
  • New work = new card, immediately. One line: Campaign | Action | Detail.
  • The checklist is what "done" means. Paste the deliverable link on the card.
  • Stuck? Park with the reason — on the client, an asset, an AM decision… then move on. Parked-with-a-reason is normal; silent idle is the only failure.
  • Before clocking out: every card honest (working / parked / submitted), and if the Tomorrow line is empty, tell your lead now.

What you get back

  • Nobody asks "what are you working on?" — it's on the screen.
  • Waiting on someone stops looking like slacking — the card says exactly who it's on.
  • Your Time today panel proves your day (client / training / internal) without a timesheet.
  • Training minutes flow in automatically from the Mastery Engine.
The daily loop · account managers

Clear your queue, then turn promises into cards

Needs your action first→Scan account health→Check commitments→Client says something→✦ Draft with AI→Edit → Create

Morning (5 min)

  • Needs your action = specialists who can't move: reviews to approve, decisions parked on you, client asks to accept/decline.
  • Health column says why in words. Red rows first, always.
  • Anyone Heavy on your accounts = a conversation with their lead today.

After any client meeting (~1 min/promise)

  • Open the client → Draft with AI → type what was agreed in plain words.
  • Each proposal suggests the person already holding that client's work, a date, an estimate, a reviewer — and warns about leave, load and stage.
  • Edit what's wrong, untick what's not needed, Create. Nothing exists until you press it.
  • Before the next meeting, the client page is the agenda.

Escalate to a department head only for exceptions: absence, overload, missing certification, changes requested twice, impossible deadline. Normal work flows AM → specialist directly.

The daily loop · COO

If it's not on the exception list, leave it alone

Open Operations→Work the list top-down→Each line: one owner, one button→Same line 2 days running → intervene→Monday: trends

What the list raises, automatically

  • Red clients (with the reason) → the AM
  • People carrying more than the team → the lead
  • On leave with due work, no cover → name cover
  • Client silent for days → the AM chases
  • Reviews waiting >24h → the reviewer
  • Changes requested twice on one person → training, not blame
  • Not trained in 14 days → the lead

Monday, 30 minutes

  • Delivery: Monitor + Throughput — on-time rate, days-to-finish, per person and per client. Trend, not number.
  • Capacity: hours + the time mix. Big "unallocated" = work off the cards.
  • Quality: first-pass approval rate; a repeating miss becomes a template checklist step.
  • Fix the system, not the incident — recurring exceptions become templates, recurrences, certifications, stages, staffing.
